FAQ

Ask Us Anythings

What does Wave CGI create?
We create creative campaigns built around your product — combining a strong concept, visual story, mixed-reality CGI, and campaign posters
What is included in a campaign?
Each campaign includes a theme, creative story, 3 mixed-reality CGI videos, and 3 connected campaign posters.
How long are the CGI videos?
Each CGI video is around 4–6 seconds, designed to create a strong visual moment that works perfectly for social media and digital advertising.
Do I need to provide the creative idea?
Not necessarily. You can give us your product and campaign goal, and we can develop the concept, theme, story, and visual direction for you.
Can you create CGI for my existing product?
Yes. We can work from your existing product photos, videos, 3D models, or physical product references and build the CGI around them.
Do you only create CGI for products?
No. We create visual campaigns for products, brands, events, launches, and promotions. Whether you want to make a product stand out or create attention around an upcoming event, Wave CGI builds the concept and visuals around your goal.
How do I start a project with Wave CGI?
Send us your product, brand details, and what you want to achieve. We'll discuss the idea, campaign direction, timeline, and next steps.
